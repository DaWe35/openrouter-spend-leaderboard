          How spend is calculated

          For each model and day: (prompt tokens − BYOK prompt tokens) × effective input price + (completion tokens − BYOK completion tokens) × effective output price.

          Effective prices are OpenRouter's measured average $/token over the last month for each model. They already account for cache-read discounts, cache-write premiums and which providers actually served the traffic. When no effective price is published, the listed price is used, with cached tokens charged at the cache-read rate. BYOK usage is excluded because it's billed by the provider, not OpenRouter. Free variants cost $0.

          These are estimates built from OpenRouter's public usage statistics, not official figures. Models billed per request, image, document or second (rerank, speech, video) are not fully covered.
